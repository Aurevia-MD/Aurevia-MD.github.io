    Request a conversationOur approach

    TRIAGE - the first minutes

    Aggregates the complicated history.

    Before the chart opens, Aurevia has already resolved the patient's longitudinal record into a structured clinical picture oriented to the upcoming encounter. The brief is personalized to the specialty, the setting, the physician's familiarity with the patient, and how the individual physician works.

    WORKUP - as results land

    The full picture for complex patients.

    Physicians can view labs, imaging, and cross-specialty reasoning all in one place. Aurevia presents the right evidence question, already framed conveniently for OpenEvidence or UpToDate.

    DISPOSITION - the decision

    A safe, actionable plan.

    Aurevia compiles the med list, flags anything that changes management, and sets up any necessary cross-specialty consultations. The physician can then make the final decision, with the patient's full record and the full picture in mind.

    TWO MODES

    Adapts as results stream in

    For routine visits, Aurevia serves as a lightweight safety net, highlighting key information and pointing out previously-missed details.

    For complex patients, Aurevia is a full-featured clinical decision support system. When a result changes the picture, Aurevia dynamically updates the information and re-routes the plan.

    SCALE & INTEGRATION

    Built for the real clinical day

    Aurevia is built to operate at health-system scale and to fit the physician's actual day. The patient state is constructed before encounters, decoupling core reasoning from real-time query load. The platform integrates through SMART on FHIR, with the engineering work on the Aurevia side—clinicians do not need to learn new systems or disrupt existing workflows. Aurevia meets physicians in the EHR they already use, with the goal of shortening time spent there, not adding to it.
